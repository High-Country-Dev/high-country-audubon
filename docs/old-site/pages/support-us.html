    What We Do

    Here are just a few of the ways we use your support on projects to help birds:
    – Native plant gardens at Valle Crucis Community Park & Green Valley Community Park
    – Collaboration in the planning & creation of wetlands at Brookshire Park & Foscoe Wetlands
    – Schools Initiative; Sue Wells Research Grant; HCAS Young Birders Scholarship
    – Adoption of a section of River Road along the New River to help keep it pristine
